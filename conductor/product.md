# Product — PCEP Rapid Practice

Zero-install, browser-based rapid-practice certification trainer.

**Commissioned by:** Rich Lysakowski, Ph.D. (NTAI) — "raw data to passed exam
in 30 days or less."
**Owner:** Commander Stephen Gill (OCNGill)

## Success criteria
1. Trainer mirrors the real exam: 30 questions / 40 minutes.
2. Answer key dual-validated (execution + independent pass), zero mismatches.
3. Repeated runs measurably cement correct answers: per-question and per-concept
   accuracy, algorithmic + user-flagged blind spots, > 90% readiness metric.
4. Every question explains why each option is right or wrong.
5. All changes verified by the headless regression suite before commit.

## Non-goals
- No server, no accounts, no telemetry — everything local (localStorage).
- No cloud anything (Gillsystems standing order).
